# WorkOps brand assets

Use **WorkOps** with this capitalization. The name describes the product; the interwoven **W–O ribbon** is its visual mark.

## Supplied marks

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| [`public/brand/workops-logo.png`](public/brand/workops-logo.png) | Primary, clean silver ribbon on black. README, product header, sign-in and documentation. |
| [`public/brand/workops-logo-alternate.png`](public/brand/workops-logo-alternate.png) | Glowing metallic treatment for larger editorial or social placements. |
| [`public/brand/source/`](public/brand/source/) | Supplied source artwork, retained alongside the prepared derivatives. |
| [`public/icons/`](public/icons/) | Application, maskable, Apple touch and favicon derivatives of the supplied ribbon. |

The primary artwork was supplied as *Glossy W-O Ribbon Logo(1).png*; the alternate as *Metallic W and O Ribbon Monogram.png*. Preparation trims empty canvas, preserves aspect ratio and adds safe padding. It does not redraw, recolor or invent a substitute monogram. The original black background is intentional; these are not claimed to be transparent assets. See the [asset manifest](public/brand/asset-manifest.json).

## Presentation

- Keep the ribbon's proportions. Do not stretch a wide mark into a square; use the square icon derivatives instead.
- Preserve the silver-and-black artwork. Use a black tile on light surfaces rather than applying a color filter or pretending the background is transparent.
- Leave clear space around the mark; keep the maskable icon's safe-zone padding.
- Use meaningful alternative text, such as `WorkOps`, when the image identifies the product. Decorative repetitions should use empty alternative text.
- Use actual application screenshots with synthetic data. Do not add fabricated AI responses, customer names, adoption figures or endorsements.
- Identify forks and modified distributions clearly so readers do not mistake them for the upstream project.
